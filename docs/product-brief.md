# TrailArc — Product Brief

Distilled from the founder's 2025 concept work (pitch decks, investor prospectus,
business model canvas, revenue models, INEOS proposal, Lovable/React prototypes).
This is the source of truth for *what* we are building; update it as decisions change.

## One line

The trusted gateway "from the driveway to the dirt" for the **middle 80% of 4×4
owners** — people who bought capable vehicles but don't know where they can
legally go, worry about damaging them, and have no one to go with.

Tagline candidates: "Technology for the Adventurous", "Your Adventure. Augmented."
Earlier working name: *The Outfitters* (now used for user ranks, e.g. "Veteran Outfitter").

## Three-sided marketplace

| Side | Pain | TrailArc gives them |
|---|---|---|
| Explorers (4×4 owners) | Where is it legal? What if it goes wrong? Who do I go with? | Legal trail data + offline maps, bookable private land, skills, badges, community |
| Landowners / hosts | Want income from land but fear liability | Turn-key listings: waivers, guest vetting, per-booking insurance, payouts |
| OEMs & brands (INEOS first) | Relationship ends at the sale | Co-branded events/challenges, anonymized usage insights, dealer service referrals |
| Agencies & NGOs (later) | Stewardship and access planning | Data dashboards, stewardship programs |

## Product pillars

1. **Trail discovery & legality** — public data (USFS MVUM, BLM, state), ownership
   boundaries, seasonal closures, green/blue/black difficulty, offline downloads.
2. **Private-land marketplace** — "Airbnb for ranch trails": price, capacity, rules,
   calendar; bookings with digital waivers, ID verification, $1–2M liability coverage.
3. **Gamification** — points, badges (trail, skill, stewardship, partner, secret
   e.g. Bigfoot), ranks Newbie → Trail Scout → Seasoned Explorer → Veteran Outfitter.
4. **Skills & AR** — step-by-step skills challenges (winching, hills, mud); camera-based
   AR first, headset VR deferred (expert-panel advice).
5. **Garage** — vehicle profile, readiness, maintenance, loadouts/packing lists.
6. **Community** — clubs, events, guided overland weekends, trip reports, forums.
7. **Adventure Flywheel** — pre-trip (plan, prep, permits) → trip (guided/self-guided)
   → post-trip (share, dealer service, next trip). Modeled on Land Rover Alexandria's
   guided-trip program (Tony Brooks).

## Business model (as drafted)

- Subscriptions: Premium $49/yr, Elite $99/yr (also drafted as "Access+" $99–199).
- Booking commission: ~15–20% private land, ~15% events, 20–30% guided trips.
- OEM & brand partnerships, sponsorships, affiliate gear sales.
- Later: data/analytics licensing, API/SaaS white-label, insurance add-on commissions.

## Go-to-market

- Pilot region: **Virginia** (INEOS U.S. strategy, VEDP incentives, local clubs).
- Year-one targets: 10K+ active users, ~600 land partners, 25M+ acres mapped,
  1 OEM + 4 brand partners, $1.5–2M revenue.
- Acquisition loops: creators/influencers, clubs (Jeep/Bronco/4Runner/Land Rover/
  Grenadier), OEM & dealer (90-day Elite with each new 4×4).
